# NNNN. Title in the form of the decision

- Status: Proposed | Accepted | Rejected | Superseded by NNNN
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Deciders: GitHub logins, comma-separated
- Serves: the epic, card or issue it serves (#N, C12)

## Context

What forces the decision: the problem, the constraints, what we know.

## Options

What we could do, with what each costs and brings. Optional for a small decision.

## Decision

What we do, stated so that a reviewer can check a change against it.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what we must now do or watch.
